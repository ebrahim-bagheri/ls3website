Ebrahim Bagheri is a Professor and PhD Program Director at the Faculty of Information,
University of Toronto, and directs LS3. He is an interdisciplinary researcher working on
efficient and responsible information retrieval.

Before joining the Faculty of Information he held a Canada Research Chair in Social Information
Retrieval and an NSERC Industrial Research Chair in Social Media Analytics at Toronto
Metropolitan University, where he founded and directed an NSERC CREATE initiative on
responsible AI. He received the 2019 Government of Canada NSERC Synergy Award for Innovation.

He serves as an Associate Editor for *IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering* and
*ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology*.
